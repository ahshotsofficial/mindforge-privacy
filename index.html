Mindforge is a personal content pipeline that generates a 60-second psychology explainer every morning, adds natural AI narration, renders a finished vertical video — and uploads it to your own YouTube channel as a private video for your review.

How it works

Four steps run automatically on GitHub Actions — no daily manual work.

Step 1

Generate

A content generator picks a fresh psychology topic (never repeating within its cycle) and writes the script: hook, misconception, mechanism, evidence and a call to action.

Step 2

Narrate

The script is converted into natural AI speech, synchronized to the five scenes of the video. Scene directions and hashtags are never spoken.

Step 3

Render

An animated stickman video is rendered at 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps, exactly 60 seconds — ready for the YouTube Shorts feed.

Step 4

Upload privately

The finished video is uploaded via the official YouTube Data API to the owner's channel with privacyStatus: private. You review it and decide if and when it becomes public.

Privacy-first by design

Mindforge automates content creation — it never collects audience data and never publishes on its own.

  • Nothing publishes automatically. Every video lands as private. Making a video public is always a manual decision by the channel owner.
  • Minimum permissions. The pipeline requests exactly one Google permission — youtube.upload — and nothing else. It cannot read your history, comments, subscriptions or channel analytics.
  • Credentials stay in GitHub Secrets. OAuth tokens are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, used in memory during the daily run, and are never logged, committed or shared.
  • No audience data collection. The public website sets no cookies, loads no trackers and collects no analytics.
Have a question about how Mindforge handles data? See the Privacy Policy or contact the owner at the email listed in the policy footer.

Why "private upload"?

Full automation is useful; full autonomy is risky.

Mindforge can produce a finished Short every day without supervision — but publishing to a public audience is a judgment call that belongs to a human. That's why the final step of the pipeline stops at private: the video waits in YouTube Studio for the owner's review. Promoting it to public visibility is a manual click, made only after quality, accuracy and tone have been checked.
